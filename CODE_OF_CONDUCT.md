# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

At Yukti — AI Teaching Assistant, we are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who participates in our project and community.

We believe that collaboration, constructive feedback, and diverse perspectives help us build better educational technology.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate toward all contributors.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them understand the project.
- Providing constructive feedback and accepting feedback gracefully.
- Respecting different viewpoints, experiences, and skill levels.
- Focusing on what benefits the project and its users.
- Communicating professionally in issues, pull requests, and discussions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, trolling, or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Submitting knowingly malicious code or intentionally compromising project security.
- Any behavior that creates a hostile or unsafe environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and other interactions associated with Yukti.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove inappropriate content, reject contributions, or take other reasonable corrective action when necessary.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer through the repository's GitHub contact channels.

Please provide relevant details so the concern can be reviewed appropriately. Reports will be handled with discretion to the extent reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports fairly and determine appropriate action based on the nature and severity of the conduct.

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and adapted for the Yukti project.
